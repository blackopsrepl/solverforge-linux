# GTK apps (Thunar and the rest) theme through Breeze, not Adwaita: the palette
# in gtk-colors.css overrides the *_breeze color names that Breeze aliases, while
# Adwaita's widget rules hardcode their hex values. kf6-breeze-icons supplies the
# breeze-dark icon set gtk{3,4}-settings.ini names.
gtk3-metatheme-breeze6
gtk4-metatheme-breeze6
kf6-breeze-icons
